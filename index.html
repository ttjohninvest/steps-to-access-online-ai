STEPS TO USING ANY OF THE AI MODELS ON THE INTERNET
1. Open https://www.requesty.ai/
2. Click the start for free button.
3. Click model library or Approved.
4. From the model names list, click the copy model id icon, ⿻, to put it on the clipboard.
5. In google.com, paste model id name you just copied.
6. Click on the company name that supports the model.
7. Follow steps to use the model. Provide prompts and get responses from the ai.
